# V18: creature balance and Legendary upgrades

2026-11-08. Full development base: V17. Creature Review03p rebuilt from V18. Production Recovery07 modern/classic includes only the Legendary lookup fix where required.

## Requested creature changes

- Black Spider (`nspb`) removed from Guardia Forest's night spawn pool and Forest Devour eligibility. It was already absent from the day pool. Both Highroad day/night pools retain it. Arena teams, loot and spider hunt eligibility are unchanged. Review shows only the Highroad Black Spider (localized `rSP3`) and its existing Battle Arena copy; the obsolete Forest exhibit is removed rather than moved into the unassigned section.
- `ngnv` Whirlwind of Steel: **300 physical damage each second**, down from 500. Existing 250 radius, 5-second duration, mana/cooldown and difficulty scaling remain. Ability and copied creature tooltips updated.
- `ngnw` Aerial Assault: **120 wind-elemental physical damage per bounce**, down from 150. Existing five bounces, mana/cooldown and difficulty scaling remain. Ability and copied creature tooltips updated.
- Removed the stack badge feature completely: eight D7_Badge functions, badge globals, selection/load event registration/frame creation and heartbeat draw/init calls. Native status icons and all underlying stack gameplay are retained. The independent review-collision setting and difficulty scalar remain.

## Legendary audit and correction

Shrine choices are encoded as mastery ability ranks: 4 = +10 stats, 5 = Last Stand, 6 = Focus, 7 = Adrenaline, 8 = Serenity, 9 = Spellbreaker. The shrine removes the old passive/adds the new one, and cached job activation restores the appropriate passive. Those transitions already worked.

The modern equipment-aware combat lookup classified all five passive abilities as equipment effects and could return zero despite their native presence on the hero. `Forsaken_ElementAbilityLevel` now recognizes a present passive matching the hero's selected mastery rank as innate before scanning equipment. No new table, timer, cache, event, inventory mutation or owner-local branch is added. Other item-granted versions retain their existing lookup. Classic already uses native ability queries and needs no equivalent gameplay change.

Real damage-step tests confirm these existing effects:

| Upgrade | Existing effect now recognized |
| --- | --- |
| Last Stand | 50% incoming damage reduction at 30% HP or lower, subject to the existing pure/ignore-defense/healing exclusions. |
| Focus | Physical damage x1.5 at 99% HP or higher. |
| Adrenaline | Physical damage x2 at 30% HP or lower. |
| Serenity | Magic damage x1.5 at 99% HP or higher. |
| Spellbreaker | Magic damage x2 at 30% HP or lower. |

The save format remains unchanged. It records mastery tier, **not the selected shrine upgrade**, so loading a Legendary job restores the default +10-stats choice. Preserving a selected upgrade across saves was asked as an optional separate scope; no answer was received before completing the recommended unchanged-format pass. No new save/loading behavior was silently introduced.
