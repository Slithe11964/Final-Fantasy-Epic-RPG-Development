# V2 creature update

Built from V1. Development map only; production 0.9.7.5g, its 1.29.2 build, editor master, V1 and earlier tests remain preserved. Next full test name is V3.

## Corrections

- Green Evasive Endurance only on Forest Goblin Great Shaman; purple Abyssal Brilliance only on Naga Sea Witch; actual Thorns art only on Corrupted Tree of Life. Aura gameplay buffs remain on allies, without recipient aura art.
- Sea Witch Vortex uses a blue, flattened water-vortex effect instead of the portal explosion.
- Splintering Images cooldown 60 seconds. Images expire at 30 seconds without inheritance; enemy kills before expiration grant the original bonuses. Removed images grant nothing.
- Protector has a predeclared ranged weapon at 1000 range while rooted; remains rooted until manually uprooted or its target escapes and no other target is in range. It can reposition and root again. Uprooted melee remains 351-362; rooted attack 451-512. All three ancients explicitly regain Move and their prior movement speed on uprooting.
- Life of the Forest cannot build. Cover Archers is an activated 30-second effect with a 45-second cooldown, 800 range and 50% damage sharing; AI can cast while rooted.
- Brood Shell is a ten-second egg timer attached to Mother Spider, without a separate shell unit, shield or health pool. Killing Mother early cancels the extra egg wave; her ordinary death split still produces three Broodlings.
- Permanent Broodlings: level 40, 2500 HP, 39 armor, 173-196 damage. Each landed attack has 35% chance to add an independent five-tick poison stack, each tick 100 + 5 x victim hero level. Each Broodling splits into two permanent Spiderlings.
- Permanent Spiderlings: level 30, 1000 HP, 25 armor, 125-134 damage. Death permanently adds +5% attack speed to allied Mother Spiders/Broodlings within 1000, stacking subject to Warcraft's attack-speed cap. Other types receive nothing.
- Infernal uses native Inferno: 1.5-second meteor landing delay before damage/summoning, 2000 fire damage, 60-second summoned lifetime.

## Icy Realm

Requested Normal stats applied to Queen, Shaman, Berserker, Wendigo, Whelp, Troll, Priest, Elder and Great Polar Bear. Monster-only spell definitions preserve player/shared spells.

Queen Devour is restricted to the existing Icy Realm day/night pool, injured friendly creatures, two uses and 20-second cooldown; +40 evasion. Winter Eclipse is a 1000-long, 300-wide cold projectile: 3-second enemy stun and 2500 ally healing once per target, without unspecified direct damage. Shaman Regen restores its maximum HP over 30 seconds. Blood Duel persists while caster and victim live and remains removable by Gaya's Break Stun; +50% damage, +60% attack speed and +10% incoming damage during the duel. Stone stuns 4 seconds/3000 damage. Frost Breath deals 3000 initial +500/s for 5 seconds. Ice Beam adds independent -20% attack-speed stacks per second, each lasting 30 seconds. Troll Alert counts down 30 seconds and doubles zone-8 frequency/amount for 60 seconds. Priest uses autocast Shell plus Blind/Fog/Pain with the existing effects. Elder slams 300 then 500 radius after 2 seconds for 2000 ice and 3-second stun per hit; its roar gives +60% attack speed within 800 for 20 seconds. Bear has 25%/700-ice Pulverize, 3000-damage healing/regen-impairing Bite, and a real charge followed by three seconds of rapid attacks with +500 ice per landed attack.

## Dark Dragon Marsh

Requested Normal stats applied; unspecified HP, levels and attack damage preserved. Black Dragon gains injured-friendly regional Devour (two uses/20 seconds), 800-range 1% physical/5% spell lifesteal aura, 5000-omni cone, 20-second Elemental Shell and 2000-omni fireball/3-second stun. Shell provides an additional multiplicative 25% reduction to elemental damage, retaining immunity/absorption. Dusk Wyrm lightning cone, Nether Drake wind cone and Marsh Whelp fire cone deal 4000 damage with 10-second cooldowns. Breath cones are 800 long, widening from 150 to 300. Crawler Sacrifice below 3000 HP heals other allies within 600 for 5000, plays blood death, and uses a tiny guaranteed player-credited killing hit so normal experience, Gaya and banking rules apply. Toxic Plague deals 500/s and reduces affected enemies' accuracy by 40.

Where mana/cooldowns were unspecified, explicit test defaults are shown on each spell tooltip. Marsh Whelp Fire Breath uses 200 mana. No new always-on timer: timed effects reuse the existing synchronized 1Hz monster heartbeat, charges reuse the existing active-only charge timer, and missiles use the existing missile engine. Image expiration uses one-shot timers. No owner-local gameplay mutations were added.
